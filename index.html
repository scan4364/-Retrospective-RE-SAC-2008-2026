RESEARCH QUESTION 04

RE phases supported

What phases of the RE process are supported by the contributions?

The categories follow Kotonya and Sommerville’s requirements engineering process. Requirements management is also treated as an RE phase.

The balance among RE phases changed. Specification—the dominant phase in P1—falls to third place in P2. Elicitation and Analysis & Negotiation each receive 35 labels, equivalent to 52.2% of the 67 P2 items. Requirements Management increases from six to 15 labels, while Validation remains nearly constant (13 and 12 labels).
